About this project

MedDetect AI is a bachelor's project that brings seven detectors under one interface. It comes with a training and evaluation pipeline that compares each model with a retrained baseline on the same data, so their accuracy can be measured rather than assumed.

Every detector here was trained on a small public dataset and has known limits, listed on its page. Use it to explore what these models can and can't do, not to make decisions about anyone's health.
